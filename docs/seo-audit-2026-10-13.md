## 2. Technical crawl (all 123 URLs and 83 other internal links)

- **Clean:**
  - every page returns 200, with no redirect chains;
  - canonical equals the URL on every page;
  - no noindex (meta or header);
  - exactly one H1 and at least one H2 on every page;
  - every image has alt text;
  - JSON-LD parses on every page: Organization and WebSite everywhere, FAQPage on 106 pages, BreadcrumbList on 117;
  - no duplicate titles or descriptions.
- One internal link is a 404: `/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection`. It is Cloudflare's e-mail obfuscation link, harmless and expected.
- **Long titles:** 30 are over about 62 characters and get cut in results, usually only the "— easyPhoto" suffix. Low priority: rewriting 30 titles at once is the many-page change CLAUDE.md §2 warns against.
- **Long descriptions:** 20 are over 165 characters (longest: /canada-passport-photo/ 270, /aadhaar-photo/ 231). Google shortens these; low priority.
- **Weak internal linking:** /aadhaar-photo/ and /convert/ each have only 1 internal link pointing to them.
- **Heavy HTML:** the homepage is 485 KB of HTML (/tools/ 335 KB). Speed is still good (§3), so no action now.

New candidates:

- **A. Voter ID blog vs Voter ID page (cannibalisation).**
  - Both pages compete for "voter id photo size" and "voter card photo size 4.5 × 3.5". Google ranks the blog at 8–9 and the Voter ID page at 7–8.
  - Fix: one plain link near the top of the blog post to the Voter ID page ("resize your photo for Voter ID Form 6"), so the page with the tool is the clear answer.
  - Body text only; no title change.
- **B. "MRP size photo" lands on the Nepal page.**
  - About 280 impressions at positions 4–7 and 0 clicks ("mrp size photo", "mrp photo size"). In Nepal, "MRP size" is the everyday name for a passport-size photo.
  - The page's 35 × 45 mm is *not confirmed* by Nepal's Department of Passports, and its e-passport photo is taken live (lib/countrySpecs.ts).
  - Under CLAUDE.md §3 the snippet can't present 35 × 45 mm as the official size. Do this only if an official Nepali source is found; otherwise skip.
- **C. "DPI should be 200" on the DPI tool.**
  - 72 impressions at position 5 for the exact phrase that forms print, plus about 150 for "dpi 200" variants.
  - Fix: one H2 in those words: "My form says DPI should be 200".

## 6. Not worth chasing

- **Image-intent searches.** "driving licence image", "pan card image 2026", "oci card image": people want to see a card, not use a tool.
- **Generic head terms.** "white background generator" (position 16) and "online photo scanner": dominated by large global tools.
- **New pages.** The index stays frozen (CLAUDE.md §1).
