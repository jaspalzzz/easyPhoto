# easyPhoto affiliate shortlist — July 2026

> **Research date:** 12 July 2026. Affiliate terms, merchant availability, rates, cookies, approval rules and payout methods change frequently. **Re-verify every entry on the linked official program or network page before signup or placement.** “Not published” below means the term could not be verified publicly; it does not mean there is no term.

## Decision summary

easyPhoto should start with **Testbook’s direct Associate Program**. It is the cleanest audience match for the 96% India traffic: the same visitors reading SSC, Railway, Banking, Defence and Teaching exam-photo requirements are plausible buyers of test series and courses. The official program explicitly accepts individuals, pays Indian bank accounts from a low ₹250 threshold, uses a 30-day attribution window and advertises up to 30% per sale.

The second test should be **iVisa**, but only on Tier-1 visa-photo pages and India-outbound visa content, with a conspicuous disclosure that it is an independent paid document-processing service—not a government site. The third should be **Zoomin through INRDeals**, positioned only after a user has created a print-ready passport photo. It is the most natural print-and-deliver offer found, although users upload the finished photo to Zoomin, so the hand-off must be clearly separated from easyPhoto’s no-upload workflow.

### Top three

1. **Testbook — best first pick.** Highest combined intent match and India payout feasibility; direct program, individual-friendly, bank payout, ₹250 threshold, 30-day attribution and up to 30% CPS.
2. **iVisa — best high-value vertical.** Excellent match for visa-photo traffic, up to 20% of service fees and a 365-day first-click cookie; India eligibility and Wise/PayPal practicality should be confirmed before launch.
3. **Zoomin via INRDeals — best print follow-on.** Directly answers “I made the photo; now I need prints,” with Pan-India fulfillment and up to 24% CPS advertised by INRDeals; keep it off private-tool workspaces until after download.

## Candidate details

### 1) Testbook Associate Program — recommended first launch

- **What it sells:** Testbook Pass subscriptions, online test series and courses for SSC, Railways, Banking, Engineering, Defence, Insurance, Teaching and other Indian competitive exams.
- **Intent match:** Place a restrained “Prepare for this exam” module on the matching `/exam-requirements/{exam}/` page and relevant exam guides. A visitor preparing an SSC application photo is plausibly preparing for SSC; no behavioral leap is required.
- **Commission:** Up to **30% per completed sale** for courses and Testbook Pass products.
- **Attribution:** **30 days from first interaction** through the associate link; official page says web, app and mobile-web transactions count.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** India-first. The official page explicitly describes the program as open to **individuals, small educational institutes, and marketing/sales people**, with a free application reviewed in 24–48 hours.
- **Network:** Direct Testbook Associate Program.
- **Payout:** Indian **bank transfer**; weekly or monthly. Official FAQ states weekly release when unpaid confirmed commission is **₹250 or more**.
- **Signup:** [Official Testbook Associate Program](https://testbook.com/associates)
- **Why 5/5:** Best overlap with easyPhoto’s dominant audience, strong public terms and the easiest verified India payout path.
- **Trust note:** Keep promotions exam-specific. Avoid “best coaching,” selection guarantees, countdown pressure, or ads inside the upload/result workspace.

### 2) iVisa Affiliate Program — high-value, tightly scoped pilot

- **What it sells:** Paid visa, eVisa and travel-document processing assistance. The commission applies to iVisa’s **service fee**, not government fees.
- **Intent match:** Tier-1 visa-photo makers and destination visa guides; also India-outbound traffic after it has checked photo requirements. Do not place it on exam/signature/PDF utility pages.
- **Commission:** **Up to 20% of service fees**.
- **Attribution:** **365-day cookie**, **first-click attribution**; repeat purchases during the cookie period are commissionable according to the official program page.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** The service and signup are global and the form accepts a person’s name, website and traffic source. The public terms do **not explicitly confirm India-resident publishers or unregistered sole individuals**, so email the partner team before relying on acceptance.
- **Network:** Direct iVisa program.
- **Payout:** Monthly on the **15th** for completed prior-month sales; **US$50 minimum**. iVisa’s affiliate academy says **PayPal or Wise/wire transfer**.
- **Signup:** [Official iVisa affiliate page](https://www.ivisa.com/affiliates) · [official affiliate terms](https://www.ivisa.com/affiliate-terms-and-conditions)
- **Why 5/5:** The strongest high-AOV extension of visa-photo intent, with unusually long attribution.
- **Privacy/trust flag:** **High disclosure requirement.** iVisa collects passport and application data on its own service. CTA copy should say “Continue to independent visa-processing service” and explain that easyPhoto’s local-processing promise ends when the user follows the external link. Never imply iVisa is an embassy or government service.

### 3) Zoomin via INRDeals — best print-and-deliver fit

- **What it sells:** Online photo prints and personalized photo products delivered across India.
- **Intent match:** A post-download CTA such as “Need physical copies? Order prints” on passport-photo guides/result follow-ups. This is the closest commercial continuation of easyPhoto’s core job.
- **Commission:** INRDeals advertises **up to 24% per delivered sale**; this is a ceiling subject to volume and advertiser approval, not a guaranteed base rate.
- **Attribution:** INRDeals does not publish the Zoomin cookie on the campaign page. A separate Indoleads listing reports one day, but network terms can differ; **verify inside INRDeals before launch**.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** Pan-India campaign. INRDeals accepts websites, blogs, apps and social publishers; its publisher signup shows **company name as optional**, supporting individual signup subject to approval.
- **Network:** **INRDeals aggregator**, whose single signup covers hundreds of merchants/campaigns.
- **Payout:** Bank transfer, UPI or Paytm after **₹500** validated earnings. Zoomin validation is listed as **45–60 days**; INRDeals says withdrawals are processed after merchant confirmation and advertises payout within seven days once payable.
- **Signup:** [Zoomin campaign on INRDeals](https://inrdeals.com/campaigns/zoomin-affiliate-program) · [INRDeals publisher signup](https://inrdeals.com/signup)
- **Why 5/5:** Perfect “digital file → physical copies” progression for Indian passport-photo users.
- **Privacy/trust flag:** **Material but manageable.** Zoomin necessarily receives the photo for printing. Place the offer only after the easyPhoto download, label it external, and never suggest the print provider shares easyPhoto’s no-upload architecture.

### 4) VisaHQ — relevant but commercially opaque

- **What it sells:** Visa application processing and travel-document services.
- **Intent match:** Destination-specific visa-photo pages where the user may need application assistance next.
- **Commission:** VisaHQ says it pays commission for online and offline visa applications, but **does not publish a rate**.
- **Attribution:** Referral-link tracking is stated; cookie/window is not public.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** Global-facing, but India-resident and unregistered-individual eligibility are not stated publicly.
- **Network:** Direct.
- **Payout:** Method, frequency and minimum threshold are not published.
- **Signup:** [VisaHQ affiliate/partner solution](https://visahq.net/) · [VisaHQ affiliate overview](https://www.visahq.de/en/partnership.php)
- **Why 4/5:** Strong audience relevance, but insufficient public terms make it non-decision-ready until written answers are obtained.
- **Privacy/trust flag:** Same external-data handoff risk as iVisa; use explicit third-party processing language.

### 5) sherpa° eVisa partner program — strong product, likely B2B onboarding

- **What it sells:** eVisas/eTAs and travel-requirement tools through hosted, co-branded and embeddable products.
- **Intent match:** Deep-link from a destination visa-photo page with passport nationality and destination prefilled. That is more precise than a generic travel banner.
- **Commission:** Negotiated **revenue share**; no public percentage.
- **Attribution:** `affiliateId` in deep links is required for commission; public docs do not specify a cookie window.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** Global partner product used in many countries, but public material is aimed at travel companies. Acceptance of an India-based individual publisher is unknown.
- **Network:** Direct sherpa° partnership.
- **Payout:** Not published.
- **Signup/contact:** [sherpa° WebApp affiliate documentation](https://docs.joinsherpa.io/webapp/quickstart) · [eVisa revenue-share overview](https://www.joinsherpa.com/products/evisa-ancillary-revenue)
- **Why 4/5:** Excellent intent routing, but bespoke B2B economics and onboarding reduce near-term feasibility.
- **Privacy/trust flag:** sherpa° states that application identity documents are entered directly into its hosted environment. Explain the handoff and do not embed it inside easyPhoto’s private tool flow.

### 6) Atlys — partnership lead, not a verified affiliate program

- **What it sells:** Visa application and tracking services, with strong India-outbound relevance.
- **Intent match:** India-origin visa-photo and study-abroad pages.
- **Commission / attribution / payout:** **Not published.** No public self-serve affiliate terms were found.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** India relevance is strong, but the public partner page showcases airlines, banks, fintech, study-abroad and lifestyle brands. It does not say an individual publisher can join.
- **Network:** Direct bespoke partnership.
- **Contact:** [Atlys partnerships](https://www.atlys.com/partners) (`partnerships@atlys.com` is listed there).
- **Why 4/5:** Great audience fit, poor immediate feasibility. Treat as a business-development lead only after demonstrating visa-page traffic.
- **Privacy/trust flag:** External application service collecting travel documents; require the same clear handoff disclosure.

### 7) Testbook via INRDeals — approval fallback

- **What it sells / intent match:** Same Testbook courses and test series as the direct program.
- **Commission:** INRDeals advertises **up to ₹105 per sale**, subject to volume and advertiser approval.
- **Attribution:** Not publicly stated on the INRDeals campaign page.
- **GEO / publisher eligibility:** India campaign; INRDeals supports website/blog/social publishers and its publisher form does not require a company name.
- **Network / payout:** INRDeals aggregator; bank, UPI or Paytm; **₹500** minimum; campaign validation **45–60 days**.
- **Signup:** [Testbook campaign on INRDeals](https://inrdeals.com/campaigns/testbook-affiliate-program)
- **Why 4/5:** Operationally easy, but direct Testbook offers a published 30-day window, up to 30%, and a lower ₹250 threshold. Use only if direct approval fails or tracked economics prove better.

## Placement and trust guardrails

1. **Keep offers outside the editing workspace.** Show them after download or in editorial/requirements content, never beside upload, crop, signature or document-preview controls.
2. **Disclose affiliate compensation clearly** before or next to the first commercial link.
3. **Preserve the privacy boundary.** For print and visa partners, state that the external provider will receive files/data the user submits and has its own privacy policy. easyPhoto’s “nothing uploaded” promise applies only while using easyPhoto.
4. **Never imply government affiliation or guaranteed acceptance.** Visa processors are optional paid intermediaries; always link the official authority as the primary requirement source.
5. **Deep-link narrowly.** Testbook links should match the exam; visa-service links should match nationality/destination; print links should appear only after a print-ready asset exists.
6. **Measure a small pilot.** Start with one Testbook module, one iVisa destination cohort and one Zoomin post-download CTA. Compare outbound CTR, validated conversion, revenue per 1,000 eligible sessions, complaint rate and impact on tool completion before expanding.

## Signup verification checklist

Before activating any candidate, obtain or screenshot the current dashboard/terms for:

- India-resident individual/sole-proprietor eligibility and required PAN/GST/invoice documents;
- exact base rate versus “up to” ceiling, excluded products, returns and chargebacks;
- cookie duration and first/last-click attribution;
- deep-link availability and whether app conversions track;
- validation delay, payout schedule, currency, threshold, bank/UPI/Wise/PayPal fees and TDS;
- trademark, paid-search, email, coupon and disclosure restrictions;
- data-processing handoff language suitable for easyPhoto’s privacy promise.
